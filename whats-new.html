Release notes

What's new in Locale

Every improvement we ship, in one place. We're in beta and moving fast — thanks for coming along.

August 1, 2026

Your plan, protected

Add a booking without losing your plan

  • 📎
    Attach a booking, and that's all that happens

    Upload a flight, train, hotel or event ticket and Locale files it on the right day. A train lands in your travel pills and on its departure day, linked to the operator. A hotel adds check-in and check-out. Your itinerary is never rewritten.

  • 🚄
    Trains, buses, ferries and tickets

    Confirmations are no longer flights-and-hotels only. Eurostar, Amtrak, coach, ferry and event tickets are all understood.

  • ⚠️
    Rewriting is now a deliberate choice

    Regenerating the whole itinerary is tucked away, clearly marked, and tells you exactly how many stops it would delete before it does anything.

  • ✍️
    No more retyping your flight

    Adding travel on the Crew tab? Upload the confirmation and the form fills itself in. Everything stays editable.

August 1, 2026

Sign-in & sync

Sign in with a code, and your trips stop disappearing

  • 🔢
    A code instead of a magic link

    Some email providers scan links before you can click them, which quietly used up single-use sign-in links. A code you type can't be consumed by a scanner, so crew invites work on Outlook and Hotmail too.

  • 🛟
    Trips built before you sign in are kept

    Plan first, sign in later. Your trip is now claimed to your account before anything syncs, closing a path where a locally-built trip could be overwritten.

August 1, 2026

Days & itineraries

Days that read like a real plan

  • 📅
    Move a stop to another day

    Put something on the wrong day? Open it, tap Move to day, pick the new one.

  • 🗓️
    Weekdays and dates, not "Day 1"

    Each day reads "Thursday, Sep 17". Rename any day and the date stays underneath.

  • 🔗
    Links in notes actually work

    Paste a booking link into a note and everyone can tap it.

  • 🚶
    Walking directions by default

    Mapping a day used to open driving directions. City days now open walking, one tap from transit.

  • ✨
    Sharper AI itineraries

    Named venues with what to order and when to go, instead of "explore the old town". Every stop links to the real site, and sports fans can now say so.

  • 🧳
    Next 3 things includes packing

    Your overview now counts what's left to pack as the trip gets close.

July 8, 2026

Accessibility & security

Easier for everyone to use — and safer

  • 🗣️
    Screen-reader support

    Status messages, dialogs, and navigation now announce properly for assistive tech.

  • 🎨
    Clearer contrast & focus

    Buttons and text meet accessibility contrast standards, with a visible keyboard focus outline throughout.

  • ⌨️
    Keyboard-friendly dialogs

    Pop-ups keep focus inside them and close with the Esc key.

  • 🔒
    Security & privacy hardening

    Tightened database access rules and locked-down third-party scripts, plus refreshed foundations under the hood.

July 5, 2026

Crew & sharing

Plan with your whole crew

  • 👥
    Invite your crew

    Add people as Crew (can add notes) or Viewers (read-only). The owner always stays in control of the trip.

  • ✅
    RSVPs

    See who's going, who's pending, and who's out — at a glance.

  • 🔗
    Share a trip link

    Send a link so friends can see the plan; sign in later to add their own notes and get updates.

June 2026

Budgets, calendar & flights

The details that make a trip run smoothly

  • 💸
    Shared budgets & settle-up

    Split costs unevenly (leave the toddler off the bar tab), track who paid, and settle up with Venmo/Zelle links.

  • 📅
    Add to your calendar

    Export your itinerary as a calendar file that drops straight into Apple or Google Calendar.

  • ✈️
    Tappable flight details

    Flight pills open airline check-in / manage-booking pages and live flight tracking.

  • 🗺️
    Inline maps & directions

    See each stop on a map and get one-tap directions without leaving the app.

  • 🧳
    Weather-aware packing

    Packing lists now match the season and climate of where and when you're going.

  • 🗓️
    Redesigned day timeline

    A cleaner day-by-day view with per-stop notes and who added what.
