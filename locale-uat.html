Before you start
What is Locale? A travel planning app where you can create trips, build itineraries with AI, check off prep tasks, and share plans with your group.

What we need from you: Try each task below on your phone. Check it off if it works, skip it if you get stuck, and leave a note at the bottom.
⏱ Takes about 5–10 minutes. No account needed.

Getting started
Open the app
Tap the "Open Locale" button above. It should load a home screen with trip cards.
Required
Add to home screen
iPhone: tap the Share icon → "Add to Home Screen." Android: tap ⋮ → "Add to Home Screen." Try opening from your home screen.
Optional
Create a trip
Tap the + button and create a new trip
Fill in trip name, destination, dates, and occasion. Hit Create.
Required
Your trip appears on the home screen
After creating, you should land inside the trip. Hit ← Back and confirm the card shows on the dashboard.
Required
Change the trip color
Open your trip → Overview tab → tap a color swatch. Does the card color update on the dashboard?
Optional
AI Itinerary Generator
Add the API key in Settings
Stephanie will text you an API key. Inside your trip, tap the ⚙️ Settings tab and paste it into the "Claude API Key" field. Tap Save.
Required for AI
Generate an itinerary with AI
Go to the Days tab → tap "Generate Itinerary." Describe what you want (e.g. "3 days in Paris, mix of museums and food, with kids"). Does it generate a full day-by-day plan?
Required for AI
Checklist & Days
Check off items in the Checklist tab
Open your trip → Checklist tab. Tap a few items to check them off. Do the checkboxes fill in and the progress bar update?
Required
Tap a "Book tickets" or "Book reservation" button on the checklist
Open the London + Paris demo trip → Checklist → Bookings section. Booking items like "Tower of London" or "Louvre" should show a teal button. Tap it — does it open the booking site without checking off the item?
Required
View the Days tab
Tap the Days tab. If there's an itinerary, tap each day to see activities and restaurants.
Required
Drag to reorder activities in a day
Open a day with 2+ activities. Grab the ⠿ handle on the left of a card and drag it up or down. Does the order update and stick after letting go?
Optional
Sharing
Tap 🔗 Share Trip and send the link to yourself
Inside a trip, tap the Share Trip button. Send the link to your own phone via iMessage or copy it. Does the link work?
Required
Open the shared link — does the invite banner appear?
Opening the link should show a bottom sheet: "You're invited! Add to my trips?" Tap "Add to my trips."
Required
PDF & Delete
Download the PDF
Tap ↓ PDF in the trip header. A new tab should open with a nicely styled itinerary. Does it look good?
Optional
Delete a trip
Tap the 🗑 icon in the trip header. Confirm the dialog. Does it go back to the dashboard and remove the card?
Optional
