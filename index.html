User protection

Your plans are yours. Period.

Locale was built privacy-first from day one · here's exactly how your data is protected.

✓

No passwords to steal

Sign-in is a one-time magic link to your email. There's no password to reuse, leak, or forget.

✓

Private by default, enforced by the database

Row-level security means the server itself only ever returns your own trips · or ones explicitly shared with you. Not even a buggy client can leak someone else's plans.

✓

You control who sees what

Trips are invite-only. Crew can add notes and RSVP; Viewers can only look. Only the owner can change or delete a trip.

✓

AI runs server-side, rate-limited & capped

AI requests go through a locked-down proxy · keys never touch your browser, usage is audited, and abuse is throttled per user and per network.

✓

Works offline, saved on your device

Your trips are cached locally first, so airplane mode isn't a problem · and you're never locked out of your own plans.

✓

No ads, no tracking, easy exit

Nothing is sold or shared. Export every trip as a JSON backup or PDF anytime · your data leaves with you.
